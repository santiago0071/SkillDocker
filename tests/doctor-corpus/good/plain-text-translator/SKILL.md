# Translation Rules

Always translate technical concepts into plain human language that anyone can easily understand.
Preserve the author original intent without adding unsolicited opinions or embellishments.
Keep sentence structures concise, direct, and completely free of confusing corporate jargon.
Never invent or extrapolate facts beyond what is explicitly stated in the source text.
